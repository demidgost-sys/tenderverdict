## Pronunciation and voice direction

- Neutral international English, credible technical-product demo, warm but restrained.
- Use the paired English/Russian reading guide in
  [`HUMAN_VOICE_RECORDING_GUIDE_RU.md`](HUMAN_VOICE_RECORDING_GUIDE_RU.md).
- The Cyrillic line is a pronunciation aid for the English directly above it; it is not a Russian
  translation and must not be recorded as a second version.
- “TenderVerdict”: **TEN-der VER-dict**.
- “RevenueCat”: normal English brand pronunciation; do not over-enunciate it.
- “CustomerInfo”: **customer info**.
- “macOS”: **Mac O-S**.
- No impersonation, cloned voice, whisper, trailer voice, exaggerated emotion, music, or synthetic
  sound effects.
- Because this route uses the owner's real recorded performance, no AI-voice disclosure is needed.
