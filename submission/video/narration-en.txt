Procurement teams often review one tender feed for several supplier profiles. TenderVerdict turns each notice into a clear human next step: open, verify, or skip.

The workflow begins with committed synthetic profiles and normalized notice metadata. Files stay on this Mac. A preview exposes counts, sample records, and metadata gaps before the deterministic analysis runs.

One canonical engine evaluates the same notices and review date for every profile. It returns three explainable verdicts: Open documents, Watch, or Reject. Reasons stay visible, and a human decides what to do next.

Free is a complete first-profile workflow, not a disabled demo. It includes the full queue, verdict reasoning, supplied source links, a shareable review brief, and deterministic schema-three JSON export.

Portfolio Workspace applies the same feed to up to five independent supplier profiles. RevenueCat unlocks the comparison matrix and full portfolio export, but never changes a verdict, creates a score, or makes an automatic bid recommendation.

In the verified Test Store baseline, the official Apple SDK loaded a development-only purchase sheet. A successful test outcome activated the supplier profiles plus entitlement through CustomerInfo. This was Test Store—not an App Store payment, and no real charge occurred.

For the current evaluation build, RevenueCat Judge Access grants the same entitlement without a purchase. A current refresh unlocked the open screen; Restore access, foreground return, and full process relaunch all preserved access from current CustomerInfo.

The evidence uses synthetic data, stores no usable key, and makes no production-billing claim. TenderVerdict is open-source Apache two point zero software for macOS.
