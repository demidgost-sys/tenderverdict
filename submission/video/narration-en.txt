One tender feed can mean different things for different suppliers. TenderVerdict turns every notice into a clear human next step.

Everything runs locally on this Mac with synthetic demo data. Before the review starts, the app shows what was loaded and what is missing.

Each notice gets one of three clear results: Open documents, Watch, or Reject. The reasons stay visible, and a person always makes the final decision.

The free version gives one complete supplier workflow: the full queue, visible reasons, source links, a review brief, and JSON export.

RevenueCat unlocks Portfolio Workspace: comparison and full export for up to five supplier profiles. It never changes a verdict, creates a score, or makes a bid decision.

In a verified RevenueCat Test Store run, the SDK showed a test purchase screen. CustomerInfo confirmed the entitlement was active. It was not an App Store payment. No money was charged.

For judges, RevenueCat Judge Access checks the same entitlement without a purchase. A fresh CustomerInfo check, Restore, returning to the app, and a full relaunch all kept access active.
