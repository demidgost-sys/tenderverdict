# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss technical decisions in good
faith, and make room for different levels of procurement and software experience. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate exposure of private information, and
disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

Feedback should address code, documentation, evidence, or observable behaviour rather than a
person. Procurement examples must not expose confidential submissions, personal data, or details
that could identify a non-public participant.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, code review, and public spaces where a
person is representing TenderVerdict.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-sensitive concern, open an issue describing the project space involved and the behaviour
that needs moderation. Do not repeat abusive content unnecessarily.

For a sensitive concern, open a minimal issue requesting a private reporting channel and omit names,
screenshots, and incident details until that channel is established. The maintainer may remove
content, limit participation, close a discussion, or exclude a participant when reasonably needed
to protect the project community.

Reports will be considered in good faith, but this small experimental project does not promise a
particular response time or formal appeal process.
